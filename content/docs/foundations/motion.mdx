Motion helps people follow what changed, like a list opening below a field. Keep it short and calm. It should never be the only way to understand what happened.

<MotionScale />

## When to use each

- **`fast` (100ms):** small feedback, like a list fading in or a chevron turning.
- **`short` (200ms):** small panels opening and closing.
- **`medium` (400ms):** larger changes, like a modal or a page section expanding.
- **`long` (600ms):** rare. Use only for big, one-off moments.
- **`ease-out`:** the default. Things start quickly and settle gently.
- **`ease-out-back`:** a small overshoot, for playful moments like a success check. Use sparingly.

## Reduced motion

Some people get dizzy or distracted by movement, and turn it off in their device settings. Wrap every transition and movement in `motion-safe:`, so it only runs when they haven't:

```tsx
<span className="motion-safe:transition-transform motion-safe:duration-(--duration-fast) motion-safe:ease-out" />
```

Buttons move down half a pixel while pressed. That uses `motion-safe:` too.
