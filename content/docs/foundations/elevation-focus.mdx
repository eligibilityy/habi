## Elevation

Most surfaces are flat, with a 1px `border/subtle-01` border. Only things that float above the page get a shadow.

<ShadowScale />

- **`shadow-overlay`:** menus, suggestion lists and popovers.
- **`shadow-modal`:** modals and dialogs.

## Focus

Everything interactive shows a 2px `focus/default` ring when it receives keyboard focus. Never remove it.

<FocusRingDemo />

In code, use `focus-visible:focus-ring`. The ring then appears for keyboard users, but not when someone clicks with a mouse.
