## Elevation

Shadows are soft and used with a purpose: to lift buttons and cards slightly off the page, and to show what floats above it.

<ShadowScale />

- **`shadow-button`:** primary, secondary, tertiary and danger buttons. Subtle and disabled buttons stay flat.
- **`shadow-card`:** cards. Cards keep their 1px `border/subtle-01` border too, so they stay visible on any background.
- **`shadow-overlay`:** menus, select lists, suggestion lists and popovers.
- **`shadow-modal`:** modals and dialogs.

## Focus

Everything interactive shows a 2px `focus/default` ring when it receives keyboard focus. Never remove it.

<FocusRingDemo />

In code, use `focus-visible:focus-ring`. The ring then appears for keyboard users, but not when someone clicks with a mouse.
