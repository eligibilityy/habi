## Elevation

Shadows are soft and used with a purpose: to lift buttons and cards slightly off the page, and to show what floats above it.

<ShadowScale />

- **`shadow-button`:** primary, secondary, tertiary and danger buttons. Subtle and disabled buttons stay flat.
- **`shadow-card`:** cards. Cards keep their 1px `border/subtle-01` border too, so they stay visible on any background.
- **`shadow-overlay`:** menus, select lists, suggestion lists and popovers.
- **`shadow-modal`:** modals and dialogs.

Shadows are a hint, not the only signal: anything people can press also has a label, a fill or a border, so it's still clear in high-contrast modes where shadows disappear.

## Focus

Everything interactive shows a 2px `focus/default` ring when it receives keyboard focus, so keyboard users can see where they are (WCAG 2.4.7). Never remove it. The ring is at least 4.2:1 against every background, above the 3:1 WCAG asks for.

<FocusRingDemo />

In code, use `focus-visible:focus-ring`. The ring then appears for keyboard users, but not when someone clicks with a mouse.

- **Text inputs** show focus on every click too, because people need to see where they're typing. They draw it inside the border: `focus:outline-2 focus:-outline-offset-2 focus:outline-focus-default`. Selects keep the ring while their list is open.
- **Inside a container that clips its content**, like a tab bar, draw the ring inside the element the same way, so it isn't cut off.
