## When to use each

- **`sm` (4px):** buttons, fields, cards and menus. The default.
- **`md` (8px):** tags, payment options and icon tiles.
- **`lg` (16px):** large panels and the Digital ID card.
- **`xl` (24px):** hero images.
- **`full`:** badges, radio buttons and date cells.
