## When to use

Use the footer at the bottom of every page of an agency's site or service.

## How it will look and work

- **A dark section** with the agency's logo (the light version, 40px tall), name and address on the left.
- **Three groups of links:** Services, About and Help. Include Contact us, Accessibility, Privacy policy and Freedom of Information.
- **A bottom line:** "All content is in the public domain unless otherwise stated", and optionally "Made with the Habi Design System".
- **On phones**, the groups stack.

## Accessibility

- **Links underline on hover**, and keyboard focus shows a white focus ring that's visible on the dark background.
- **Each group has a heading**, so screen reader users can tell the groups apart.
