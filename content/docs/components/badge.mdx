## Accessibility

- **Words, not color:** the label carries the meaning. Color only reinforces it, so a badge still makes sense in grayscale or to people who can't tell colors apart (WCAG 1.4.1).
- **Short labels:** one or two words. Badges don't wrap.
- **Not interactive:** badges can't be clicked or focused. If people need to pick an option, use a Tag. If it goes somewhere, use a Link.
- **Context:** add visually hidden text (`sr-only`) when the badge only makes sense next to something else, like "Status: Open".
- **Contrast:** every kind's text and background pass WCAG AA (5.3–6.8:1).
