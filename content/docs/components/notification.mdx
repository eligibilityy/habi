## When to use

Use a notification to tell people something they need to know:

- **After an action**, like "Appointment booked" or "Payment failed".
- **About their records**, like an ID that expires soon.
- **About the service**, like planned downtime.

Use something else when:

- **One answer in a form is wrong.** Show the error next to the field, with `FieldError` (see [Text input](/docs/components/text-input#validation)), so people see it where they fix it.
- **It's a short status**, like "Open" or "Closed". Use a [Badge](/docs/components/badge).

## Usage

```tsx
import { Notification } from "@/components/ui/notification";

<Notification kind="success" title="Appointment booked">
  See you on 15 March 2026 at 9:00 AM.
</Notification>;
```

## Content guidelines

- **The title says what happened**, in a few words: "Payment failed", not "Error".
- **The details say what to do next:** "Your card was declined. Try another card or pay over the counter."
- **Be specific about dates, amounts and places:** "15 March 2026 at 9:00 AM", "₱950.00".
- **Don't blame people:** "We couldn't process your payment", not "You entered the wrong card".

## Accessibility

### What Habi handles

- **Roles:** errors use `role="alert"`, which screen readers announce straight away. Other kinds use `role="status"`, announced when the screen reader is idle.
- **Not just color:** the icon and title carry the meaning, so the message is clear without color.
- **Focus stays put:** notifications don't move focus, so people aren't pulled away from what they were doing.
- **Dismiss button:** has an accessible name ("Dismiss" by default) and a 24 × 24px target.

### What you need to do

- **Show the notification when the event happens**, like after a payment. Screen readers only announce it when it's added or changed after the page loads, not when it's part of the first page load.
- **Translate `dismissLabel`** when the page isn't in English.
