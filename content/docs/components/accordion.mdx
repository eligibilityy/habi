## When to use

Use an accordion when people only need some of the sections on a page, and can tell from the headings which ones:

- **Frequently asked questions:** "What documents do I need?", "How long does it take?"
- **Requirements that depend on the person:** a section for first-time applicants and one for renewals.

Use something else when:

- **Everyone needs the content.** Show it on the page. People miss what's hidden.
- **The sections are steps people go through in order.** Ask one thing per page instead.

## How it will look and work

- **Each item** has a heading row and a panel. The heading row holds the title (Heading/XS) and a chevron. The panel holds the answer (Body/MD, secondary text).
- **Items stack**, separated by a 1px border, with a border under the last one too.
- **States:** hover tints the heading row, keyboard focus shows a 2px focus ring inside the row, and an open item turns its chevron up.

It will be built on Base UI's [Accordion](https://base-ui.com/react/components/accordion).

## Accessibility

- **The whole heading row is one button** with `aria-expanded`, so people can open an item with a click, a tap, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>.
- **Headings stay headings,** so screen reader users can jump between them.
