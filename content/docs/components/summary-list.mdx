## When to use

Use a summary list on a "Check your answers" page, before people submit or pay.

Use something else when:

- **People can't change the information.** Use a Table.

## How it will look and work

- **A title,** like "Your details", and an optional action that changes the whole group, like "Change all".
- **One row per answer:** the question, the answer and a "Change" link.
- **Long answers wrap** onto more lines, like an appointment's date and place.
- **Plain or Card style.** Use Card when one page has several groups.
- **On phones,** the question, answer and link stack.

## Content guidelines

- **Show every answer** before people submit or pay, with a way to change each one.
- **Keep the questions short:** "Given name", not "What is your given name, as it appears on your birth certificate?"

## Accessibility

- **Questions and answers are read as pairs,** so each answer is heard with its question.
- **Each "Change" link names its question** for screen readers: "Change given name".
- **On phones,** nothing gets squeezed.
