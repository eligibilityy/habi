## When to use

Use privacy consent when a service collects personal data and needs people's consent to use it, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

When the agency uses the data under a law, not consent, show the notice and link without the checkbox.

## How it will look and work

- **A title:** "How we use your personal data".
- **A short notice** in plain words: what you collect, why, how long you keep it and who sees it.
- **A link to the full privacy notice.**
- **A checkbox** that starts unchecked and covers only this purpose.
- **States:** Default, Checked and Error.

## Content guidelines

- **Say what data you collect, why, how long you keep it and who sees it,** like "NBI uses your name, date of birth and ID photo only to process your clearance. We keep them for 5 years and do not share them outside the government."
- **Don't check the box for people.**
- **Don't bundle other things into the same box,** like news and offers.

## Accessibility

- **The title is a heading,** so people can find it when they scan the form.
- **The whole checkbox row can be tapped,** and it starts unchecked.
- **The error says what to do,** with an icon and text, and is listed in the Error summary too.
- **The link says where it goes:** the full privacy notice.
