<Callout type="warning" title="For real government websites only">
  Only use the official banner on a real government website ending in .gov.ph.
  Habi is an unofficial design concept, and the banner must never make any other
  site look official.
</Callout>

## When to use

Put the official banner at the very top of every page of a government website, above the [Header](/docs/components/header), so people can tell a real government site from a fake one.

## How it will look and work

- **A dark bar** with a ".gov.ph" badge, the statement "Official PH Government website" and a chevron.
- **Opening it** shows three ways to check a site: official websites use .gov.ph, the address starts with https://, and people should never share their OTP or PIN.
- **On phones**, the three reasons stack.

It will be built on Base UI's [Collapsible](https://base-ui.com/react/components/collapsible).

## Accessibility

- **The top row is one button** with `aria-expanded`. Hover underlines the statement, and keyboard focus shows a white focus ring that's visible on the dark bar.
