## When to use

Put the official banner, sometimes called a masthead, at the very top of every page of a government website, above the site's header. People learn to look for it, so it helps them tell a real government site from a fake one.

Don't use it on sites that aren't official government services, or change its words or colors. It only works if it looks the same everywhere.

## Usage

```tsx
import { OfficialBanner } from "@/components/ui/official-banner";

<body>
  <SkipLink />
  <OfficialBanner />
  <header>…</header>
  <main id="main-content">…</main>
</body>;
```

Put it straight after the [Skip link](/docs/components/skip-link) and before the header. It's closed until people open it, and it spans the page with its contents in a 1200px column.

On computers the three reasons sit in a row. Below 640px wide, the top row lines up on the left and the reasons stack.

## Content guidelines

- **Keep the words as they are,** apart from translating them. People learn to trust the banner as it is.
- **Don't add messages to it,** like "Welcome to our new website!". Put news on the page instead.

## Props

`OfficialBanner` is built on Base UI's [Collapsible](https://base-ui.com/react/components/collapsible) and takes its root props, like `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`. It renders a `<section>`.

## Accessibility

### What Habi handles

- **A region landmark** named "Official government website", so screen reader users can find it.
- **The top row is one button** with `aria-expanded`, so screen readers say whether it's open or closed.
- **The reasons are read straight after the button** when it's open.
- **Keyboard:** <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> opens and closes it. Focus shows a white ring, so it stays visible on the dark banner.
- **Reduced motion:** with reduced motion turned on, it opens without sliding.

### What you need to do

- **Keep it first,** after the [Skip link](/docs/components/skip-link) and before the header, on every page.
- **Translate `aria-label`** along with the words when the page isn't in English.
