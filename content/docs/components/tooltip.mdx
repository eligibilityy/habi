## When to use

Use a tooltip to:

- **Name an icon-only button**, like "Copy reference number".
- **Give a brief hint** about a control.

Don't use a tooltip for anything people need. Phones have no hover, so most people on phones never see it. Never put essential information, links or actions in one: write them on the page.

## How it will look and work

- **One short line** of Body/SM text on a dark background, with an arrow pointing at the control. Longer text wraps at 240px.
- **Placement** is the side of the control it appears on: top, bottom, left or right. **Align** slides it along that side, so it stays on screen near the edges.

It will be built on Base UI's [Tooltip](https://base-ui.com/react/components/tooltip).

## Accessibility

- **It appears on hover and on keyboard focus**, after a short delay.
- **It stays open while the pointer is over it**, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it (WCAG 1.4.13).
- **The words must exist elsewhere too.** For an icon-only button, put them in its `aria-label` as well.
