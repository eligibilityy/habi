## When to use

Use the header at the top of every page of an agency's site or service, below the [Official banner](/docs/components/official-banner).

## How it will look and work

- **Agency lockup:** the logo, then "Republic of the Philippines" above the agency's name. Logos are 40px tall on desktop and 32px on phones, and two can sit side by side.
- **Navigation:** the site's main sections. A bar under each item shows hover and the current section.
- **Language switch:** English | Filipino.
- **Search button:** opens the site [Search](/docs/components/search).
- **On phones**, the header shows the logo, the name, search and a menu button. Navigation and the language switch move into the menu.

## Accessibility

- **The current section** has `aria-current="page"`.
- **Every link and button shows the focus ring**, and the menu button says whether the menu is open.

## Related components

- [Official banner](/docs/components/official-banner): goes above the header.
- [Search](/docs/components/search): for the site search.
